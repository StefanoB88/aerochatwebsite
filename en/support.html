Write to us at aerochat.help@gmail.com (in English or Italian). We usually reply within 48 hours.

I can't see anyone nearby

  • Bluetooth must be on: in airplane mode you have to turn it back on manually.
  • Give AeroChat the Bluetooth permission (on Android, “Nearby devices”).
  • Check that “Visible to people nearby” is on.
  • Keep the app open on both phones, less than 30 metres apart and with few obstacles in between.
  • Update AeroChat to the latest version on both phones.
  • Want to try it on your own? On the main screen tap “Try it now” and chat with the virtual passenger.

Purchases

Passes are one-time purchases with no renewal. To restore them use “Restore purchases” on the Pass screen. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.

Safety and reports

Long-press a message to report it or block its author. To forward a report: aerochat.help@gmail.com. If you are in danger, call the emergency services or alert the crew.
