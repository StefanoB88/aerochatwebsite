How it works, in 3 steps

It takes a minute. The right moment is before boarding, while you still have signal.

  1. Install and pick a nickname

    No email, no phone number. Get your travel companions to install it too, while there's still 4G.

  2. Connect with a QR code

    Scan each other's QR code or send the invite link. Or say hi to nearby people right in the app.

  3. Airplane mode + Bluetooth

    On board, turn on airplane mode and switch Bluetooth back on. Chat, play and find your friends, even at 35,000 feet.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work in airplane mode?

Yes. Airplane mode turns off cellular, but on iPhone and Android you can switch Bluetooth back on while it stays active: AeroChat only needs Bluetooth. Most airlines allow Bluetooth in flight; always follow the crew's instructions.

Do I need internet or in-flight Wi-Fi?

No. You only need internet to download the app and, if you want, to buy a Pass. Everything else goes over Bluetooth, phone to phone.

Does it work without Bluetooth?

No: Bluetooth is essential. Without Bluetooth on and another phone with AeroChat nearby, messages can't be sent.

How far do messages reach?

Between two phones, a few dozen meters depending on obstacles and crowds. With more people using AeroChat, phones relay for each other (up to 7 hops), and if the recipient is out of reach the message waits and is delivered as soon as you're close again.

Can I chat between iPhone and Android?

Yes. Both apps speak the same protocol: messages, photos, games and the group Pass work across iPhone and Android.

Can I download AeroChat once I'm already on the plane?

On iPhone, no: install it before boarding. On Android, someone who already has it can share it without internet using Quick Share from the Play Store (Profile › Manage apps & device › Share apps).

Does it drain the battery?

AeroChat uses Bluetooth Low Energy, which is designed to use little power. Usage depends on how many phones are around and how much you use it; photos and calls cost more than text.

Who can read my messages?

Only the recipients. Messages are end-to-end encrypted and never go through a server. In the Public Cabin, however, messages are visible to every passenger in range: write as you would in a public square.

How do I report or block someone?

Long-press a received message and choose "Report" or "Block". Blocking is immediate. You can forward reports to aerochat.help@gmail.com.

Does the Pass renew automatically?

No. Every Pass is a one-time purchase with no automatic renewal, handled by the App Store or Google Play.
