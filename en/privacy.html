Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy describes how the AeroChat app (iOS and Android) and the AeroChat website (stefanob88.github.io/aerochatwebsite) handle personal data. In short: AeroChat has no accounts and no message servers, and contains no tracking tools.

No account, no server

AeroChat does not ask for an email address, phone number or registration. We have no servers that receive your messages: they travel directly between phones over Bluetooth, end-to-end encrypted (X25519, Ed25519, ChaCha20-Poly1305). We cannot read, store or hand over the content of your conversations.

Data on your device

Nickname, profile colour or photo, cryptographic keys, contacts added via QR code or invite link, messages, media, games, travel statistics and reports stay on your phone, in storage protected by the operating system. You can delete them by deleting your chats or uninstalling the app.

If you scan a boarding pass, the app reads on the phone only the airline, flight number, date and, if present, the seat. This data never leaves the phone; your seat is shown to nearby passengers on the same flight only if you turn on “Show my seat to people nearby” (off by default).

What others see

People you chat with see your nickname, colour and profile photo. In the public Cabin and among people nearby, other passengers see only your nickname and an anonymous technical identifier of your device, which changes if you reinstall the app. You can stop being visible to people nearby in the settings. Intermediate phones that relay messages only see encrypted packets.

Messages written in the public Cabin are visible to all passengers in range: do not share personal information there.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth (and, on Android, “Nearby devices”): required for the mesh network.
  • Camera: QR codes, boarding pass and photos to send.
  • Microphone: voice notes and calls.
  • Photos: only the images you choose to share or save.
  • Notifications: alerts for incoming messages.
  • Nearby interaction / Ultra Wideband (compatible iPhones): the arrow of the proximity radar. The measurement happens between the two phones and is not recorded.
  • Location: only when you choose to attach it to an emergency alert or send it in a chat. It is read once and sent to the recipients you choose, never to a server.

Every permission is optional, except Bluetooth.

No tracking

The app contains no advertising SDKs, third-party analytics or tracking identifiers. Its only communication with the outside world is with the App Store or Google Play for in-app purchases, handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. We do not receive your payment details.

Reports

If you choose to forward a report by email, we receive your email address, the nickname and anonymous identifier of the reported user and the text of the message. We use them only to handle the report (legal basis: legitimate interest in the safety of the community and platform obligations) and delete them within 90 days.

Support

If you write to aerochat.help@gmail.com, we use your email address and the content of your message only to reply to you, and keep them for as long as needed to handle your request.

This website

The AeroChat website is a static site: it uses no cookies, loads no third-party fonts, scripts or resources and contains no trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA), which may log standard access data (such as IP address and browser) for technical and security reasons, kept for a limited time under its own policy (GitHub Privacy Statement). The /get page reads your phone type only in your browser to open the right store; any code in the link is passed to the store as a campaign.

Children

AeroChat is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port any data we may process (in practice: support emails and reports) by writing to aerochat.help@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority responsible for the controller (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, datenschutz-berlin.de) or with the authority of your own country. The data that stays on your phone is under your sole control.

Controller and contact

Data controller: Stefano Brandi, Berlin (Germany), the developer listed on the App Store and Google Play. For privacy questions write to aerochat.help@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy together with a new version of the app; the date at the top shows the latest revision.
