1. The service

AeroChat lets you exchange messages, photos, voice notes, calls and mini-games between nearby devices over Bluetooth, without servers and without accounts. The service is provided as is: coverage depends on the phones around you and is not guaranteed. AeroChat is not a rescue tool: the emergency alert helps you reach people close to you but does not replace emergency numbers or the instructions of the crew.

2. Conduct

You may not use AeroChat to harass, threaten or discriminate against others, to spread spam or sexually explicit, violent or illegal content, or to impersonate other people. In the public Cabin the rules shown the first time you open it also apply. Anyone who breaks these terms can be blocked by other passengers and reported.

3. Reports and blocking

Every message you receive can be reported and its author blocked with one tap. Blocking is immediate and applies on this device: messages from the blocked user are no longer shown or relayed. Reports forwarded by email to aerochat.help@gmail.com are reviewed within 24 hours and may lead to abusers being excluded from future versions of the app.

4. Purchases

The Flight Group Pass is a one-time purchase handled by the App Store or Google Play, with no automatic renewal. The 24-hour Pass and the 72-hour Pass expire at the end of their duration; the Lifetime Pass is permanent. The Pass is shared with the members of your groups for the stated duration. Passes obtained through promo codes or invite rewards are for personal use and last for the stated duration. Refunds follow Apple's and Google's policies.

5. Minimum age

AeroChat is intended for people aged 13 and over. The public Cabin connects strangers: minors should use it under the supervision of an adult.

6. Use on board and in public places

Follow the rules of the airline, ship or venue you are in on the use of Bluetooth and electronic devices, and the instructions of the crew. Do not photograph or film other people without their consent.
